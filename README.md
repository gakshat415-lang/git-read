🛒 FirstClub — Increasing 30-Day Repeat Purchase

Question: How can FirstClub increase the percentage of first-time customers who place a second order within 30 days?

Analyzed 1,185 Play Store reviews using AI-assisted thematic analysis to uncover recurring motivations, pain points, and behavioral segments. The work identified four first-time-user segments and mapped the experience from initial discovery to repeat-purchase intent.

Focus: Product Analytics · Customer Research · Segmentation · Retention

View App Review Pulse →

👗 Myntra — Wishlist → Purchase Conversion

Question: What prevents a wishlisted item from becoming a purchase within 30 days?

Analyzed 4,447 public conversations, narrowed them to 927 Myntra-relevant conversations, and synthesized the evidence into 6 opportunity themes. The analysis decomposed the journey across relevance, consideration, decision, and transaction.

Focus: User Research · Funnel Analysis · Opportunity Identification · Product Analytics

View Research Tooling →

🤖 ChatGPT — Voice Adoption for India's Next Billion Users

Problem: Voice interactions can create anxiety and premature interruptions for vernacular job seekers.

Designed Paced Mode, a user-controlled voice interaction model based on a push-to-talk interaction pattern. The case study covered segmentation, journey mapping, behavioral friction, product requirements, KPI thinking, and a working prototype.

Focus: Product Strategy · User Research · AI Products · UX

🚕 Uber — Pickup Reliability in High-Density Urban Hubs

Problem: A 2D map pin can fail to represent the physical reality of airports, business districts, and multi-level infrastructure.

Proposed a Spatial OS that shifts coordination from raw GPS coordinates toward street-aware pickup guidance using semantic landmarks, 3D spatial alerts, and visual handshakes.

Focus: JTBD · First Principles · TAM / SAM / SOM · RICE

🎵 Spotify — Making Collaborative Playlists a Default Social Behaviour

Problem: Collaborative Playlists offer social value but struggle to become a habitual behavior.

Used JTBD + First Principles Thinking to identify setup friction, lack of contribution feedback, and social risk as key experience barriers, then developed a broader product direction for making collaboration more active and rewarding.

Focus: JTBD · Behavioral Psychology · Product Experience
